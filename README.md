## **Project Introduction**

DHEIS was developed to address a practical analytical challenge: health and education conditions are often examined separately, while district-level planning can benefit from a combined view.

The system brings together district-level health and education indicators, standardizes their direction and scale, calculates separate health and education vulnerability subscores, and combines them into a single **DHEIS Priority Index**.

The resulting system is intended as **decision support**, not as a replacement for administrative judgment. The platform helps users identify districts that warrant closer review, understand whether vulnerability is relatively more health-driven or education-driven, compare districts, and inspect supporting indicators.

The final application also includes an AI-powered intelligence layer that generates concise district briefs and two-district comparison briefs using only the supplied DHEIS indicators.
